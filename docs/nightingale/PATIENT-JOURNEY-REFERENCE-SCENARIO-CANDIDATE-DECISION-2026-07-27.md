## 2. Decision outcome

Nightingale needs one cross-surface reference-scenario catalog before individual Today,
My Path, Care Team, education, discharge, or communication features can be approved.
Without that catalog, independently implemented endpoints and native screens can each pass
focused tests while producing contradictory patient outcomes at transitions such as
transfer, discharge, identity correction, or content withdrawal.

This candidate therefore specifies deterministic outcomes for the required journey
families while deliberately adding no operation. It establishes these rules:

1. **A source event is not a patient release.** Admission, transfer, procedure, test,
   result, discharge, identity, or staffing facts do not become patient-visible merely
   because a source recorded them.
2. **Every value is independently governed.** Release, freshness, uncertainty,
   correction, language, and offline behavior remain field-level decisions. A document
   timestamp cannot make every field current.
3. **Unavailable, released-empty, and denied are not interchangeable.** The patient may
   receive different safe presentation states, but authorization failures must remain
   non-disclosing.
4. **Correction replaces and retraction withholds.** A prior value is never serialized
   alongside its replacement. A retracted value, actor, reason, source, and internal
   target remain hidden.
5. **Server acceptance is not staff delivery or review.** A future communication state may
   say that the server durably accepted an exact request; it may not claim that the care
   team received, read, reviewed, or will answer it.
6. **Transitions are cross-surface.** Transfer, discharge, identity correction,
   representative expiry/revocation, sensitive denial, and retraction must affect every
   applicable surface, cache, handle, draft, and capability coherently.
7. **Accessibility is information equivalence.** Images, color, sound, gestures, reading
   level, or one presentation mode may never become the only way to obtain essential
   information or perform an allowed action.
8. **The catalog remains held.** It defines testable safety expectations but does not
   select clinical sources, approve copy, define a live API, or authorize implementation.

### 3.2 What the evidence can and cannot prove

The source material can prove that the repository contains specific candidate semantics,
legacy behaviors, gaps, tests, and governance controls. It cannot prove:

- that a clinical source is authoritative for Nightingale;
- that a patient-facing term, timing statement, result, or instruction is approved;
- that a representative relationship is legally effective;
- that a staff pool, handoff, interpreter, notification provider, or support workflow is
  operational;
- that a synthetic sequence matches a production integration;
- that a native screen is understandable or accessible to patients; or
- that any residual security or clinical risk has been accepted.

No production database, deployed application, identity provider, EHR, device provider,
patient record, staff record, source response, message body, or real identifier was used.

## 5. Detailed scenario decisions

### 5.1 Uncomplicated adult admission

The synthetic admission case starts only after a separately held identity/session flow and
an authoritative current-inpatient source establish one self context. It deliberately
starts with no released patient projection.

Expected behavior:

- encounter discovery may move from zero to one opaque context;
- Today, My Path, and Care Team remain independently not available until their content is
  released;
- a section may be `released-empty` only after a complete governed evaluation;
- the UI must explain that no released items does not mean no care is planned; and
- no diagnosis, procedure, clinician, location, or discharge fact is inferred from
  admission context alone.

### 5.2 Intra-hospital unit transfer

A transfer can invalidate location, care-team presentation, communication capability, and
accountable routing at different times. The candidate therefore rejects a client-only
location swap or silent retention of the old unit.

Required sequence:

1. reconcile the authoritative transfer and current-context state;
2. withdraw the old patient-facing location before disclosing a replacement;
3. release the new location only after its own source/release decision;
4. revalidate communication capability against the current context;
5. append a content-free reroute fact only when an eligible destination resolves; and
6. use `unresolved` instead of guessing a pool or staff owner.

The patient must not be told that a particular person received or read a message merely
because responsibility routing changed.

### 5.3 Procedure during admission

The procedure case allows only separately released plan, timing-confidence, uncertainty,
and can-change fields. It prohibits:

- treating scheduled time as actual start;
- treating elapsed time as in-progress or completed state;
- treating completion as a result or outcome;
- creating patient language from an internal code; or
- copying unreviewed perioperative or clinical prose into the patient product.

Procedure vocabulary, timing semantics, source ownership, and content still require named
clinical approval.

### 5.4 Ancillary test delay and result release

Three cases separate concerns that legacy surfaces can collapse:

1. **Released delay:** withdraw prior timing, then show only an approved delayed state,
   uncertainty, and optional updated window. Do not disclose operational blame or infer
   cancellation.
2. **Unreleased result:** a source fact may exist, but no patient-facing existence, status,
   value, interpretation, or review state is exposed.
3. **Separately released result:** an independently approved result field may appear with
   complete governance context. Its presence still does not imply clinical interpretation
   or a completed care-team discussion.

### 5.5 Pre-round question and approved response

The two communication cases encode the distinction missing from patient-visible delivery
wording:

- an exact idempotent submission can become `server accepted`;
- that state means durable server acceptance only;
- a content-free accountable routing fact is separate;
- staff projection, individual receipt, review, and response are not inferred;
- the draft clears only after the exact accepted response, not after an ambiguous result;
- a staff response is a separate immutable message and must be approved for patient
  release; and
- patient push remains held until provider, token, payload, consent, lifecycle, and native
  capability designs are approved.

Routine messaging must retain an unmistakable urgent-help escape path, but this candidate
does not approve urgent wording or monitoring claims.

### 5.6 Shift handoff

The handoff case preserves thread and message identity while accountability changes through
append-only, content-free routing facts. It requires a fresh authoritative workforce input,
effective eligibility, and a resolvable destination. If no destination is eligible, the
state is `unresolved`; neither server nor client guesses.

The patient may receive only separately approved generic status. No internal pool,
staffing detail, staff name, guaranteed response time, or read claim is exposed.

### 5.7 Changed estimated discharge date

The old estimate is atomically withdrawn before the separately released replacement is
serialized. The replacement carries its own:

- observation and release times;
- confidence and uncertainty;
- can-change flag;
- language release;
- correction state; and
- patient notice.

The patient notice does not reveal an internal reason, actor, source identifier, or blame.
An estimate is never described as a promise.

### 5.8 Discharge while a thread is open

Authoritative discharge closes the inpatient access context before another patient
mutation can succeed. The future approved lifecycle policy must decide whether the thread
is closed, omitted, or handed off to a separate portal/support product. Until that policy
exists, Nightingale:

- revokes compose capability;
- withholds inpatient surfaces generically;
- purges the context handle, sensitive cache, and volatile draft;
- rejects queued or replayed mutations; and
- makes no claim that the care team read or resolved the prior question.

### 5.9 Patient identity merge or correction

Identity correction is treated as an authorization discontinuity, not a background data
refresh. The old session family and context handles are revoked, device state is purged,
and no records are auto-unioned in the client. New access requires approved reproof and an
authoritative unambiguous link.

The response does not expose whether another identity or record exists, the merge target,
the old or corrected content, or the reason for review.

### 5.10 Representative invitation, scope, expiry, and revocation

Representative access remains entirely held. The four cases exist to prevent a future
implementation from treating partial workflow state as authority:

- invitation does not grant or reveal access;
- verification, relationship, authority/consent, operation scope, field scope, expiry,
  sensitive-service exclusions, and communication authority are separate;
- self and representative realms never share handles, sessions, caches, or drafts;
- expiry and revocation deny every disclosure and mutation before serialization; and
- revoked/expired state is generically indistinguishable from other unavailable outcomes.

The scoped case still discloses nothing because no representative model is approved.

### 5.11 Limited-English-proficiency patient with interpreter

Language preference, patient-visible content language, and interpreter workflow are
separate facts:

- each displayed field must have an approved translation or approved source-language
  release;
- absent translation is withheld or handled through an approved fallback, never
  unreviewed clinical machine translation;
- interpreter requested, scheduled, confirmed, present, or unavailable states require a
  separate authoritative source and released vocabulary; and
- selecting a language does not promise that an interpreter is confirmed.

### 5.12 Accommodation equivalence

Five cases specify minimum cross-surface equivalence:

- **Visual:** reflow, screen-reader semantics, magnification/contrast, images-hidden
  equivalence, and no color-only state.
- **Hearing:** no sound-only alert and approved captions/transcripts for any future media.
- **Motor:** supported alternative input, coherent focus, adequate targets, and no
  gesture-only action.
- **Cognitive:** consistent structure, restrained status changes, explicit uncertainty,
  one primary purpose per step, and a distinct urgent-help path.
- **Low literacy:** approved plain language that retains material meaning, plus teach-back
  semantics that never equate opening/completing a screen with understanding.

These cases are requirements for future validation, not claims that the current offline
shell or any future journey has passed full accessibility review.

### 5.13 Sensitive-data case

When an effective sensitive-service policy is absent, ambiguous, or denies access, every
field, section, thread, action, cached value, draft, and capability fails closed. The
patient or representative receives no signal that the resource or sensitive service
exists, what type it is, or why it was restricted.

This candidate does not decide which services are sensitive or what self/representative
rights apply. Those are legal, privacy, clinical, identity, and product decisions.

### 5.14 Source outage and stale projection

The outage case distinguishes incomplete evaluation from an approved empty section:

- affected sections become `not-available`, never `released-empty`;
- independently governed unaffected fields may remain;
- the last known value is not silently shown as current; and
- the outage does not imply cancellation, discharge, transfer, or no care.

The stale case is field-specific. A value may be shown stale only if an approved policy
permits it and supplies an observation time plus patient notice. Otherwise it is withheld.
One document-level freshness value cannot override field state.

### 5.15 Incorrect content retracted and corrected

Retraction and correction are separate cases:

- retraction atomically removes the value from every surface and purges any approved cache;
- no withdrawn value, internal target, actor, source, or reason is disclosed;
- a replacement appears only after independent review and release;
- the replacement carries its own complete governance context; and
- a source-free patient correction notice may be shown only after copy approval.

## 6. Cross-cutting contract and platform requirements

### 6.1 Authorization sequence

Every eventual operation must apply an operation-specific version of this sequence:

```text
active Nightingale contract operation
  -> product / facility / cohort / expiry gates
    -> Nightingale realm, principal, assurance, and current session
      -> verified self or separately approved representative authority
        -> opaque context ownership
          -> authoritative current-inpatient state
            -> operation and field capability
              -> sensitive-service and relationship rules
                -> release, freshness, uncertainty, language, and correction
                  -> race recheck before serialization or mutation
                    -> durable content-free audit
                      -> atomic no-store response or exact idempotent mutation result
```

Client navigation, cached capability, possession of a handle, prior disclosure, hidden
controls, or a response from legacy Hummingbird is never authority.

### 6.2 Non-disclosure

Every fixture requires omission of:

- source-system, patient, principal, grant, encounter, staff, and routing identifiers;
- unreleased clinical values;
- withheld resource existence and denial reason;
- internal pool/staffing detail;
- approval actors and correction targets; and
- raw patient-authored text from audit, routing, monitoring, and fixture metadata.

### 6.3 Communication and notification

The candidate permits no communication mutation or notification delivery. The scenarios
define future state meaning only:

- accepted means accepted by the server;
- staff projection, delivery, reading, review, and response remain distinct;
- exact retries must reuse operation and client-message identity;
- changed retries conflict instead of duplicating;
- routing metadata remains content-free;
- no eligible owner is `unresolved`;
- notification registration is not delivery; and
- no notification payload, provider, permission, background delivery, or deep link exists
  in Nightingale.

### 6.4 Offline, cache, and draft behavior

Every case denies offline PHI, queued mutation, and presentation of a stale value as
current. A future last-known-value feature needs a separate field policy and threat review.
Context loss, identity correction, representative expiry/revocation, sensitive denial,
discharge, and retraction require handle/cache/draft purge.

### 6.5 Audit and rollback

The catalog requires content-free evaluation, authorization, field-key disclosure,
communication-state, and withhold/correction/outage facts. It forbids raw patient values,
message bodies, and internal identifiers in those facts.

Every case requires a kill switch, release withdrawal, and relevant handle/cache/draft
purge. These requirements do not prove that monitoring, incident response, or rollback is
implemented.

## 7. Mechanical verification

The verifier fails if:

- the executable foundation gains a path or activation flag;
- any of 19 global runtime/production permissions becomes true;
- any family or case is missing, reordered, duplicated, or reassigned;
- a fixture stops being synthetic and held;
- a required approval, separate-release rule, atomic response, or no-store rule is
  removed;
- a representative or sensitive-data case exposes a surface;
- a concrete UUID, email, API path, patient/staff/source identifier key, message body, or
  clinical value enters the fixtures;
- a notification state implies delivery or server acceptance implies staff delivery/read;
- offline PHI, a queued mutation, current presentation of stale data, essential imagery,
  color-only state, or action loss becomes permitted;
- identity correction no longer requires reproof;
- source outage can present last known data as current;
- retraction stops removing content atomically;
- source files or fixture serialization drift; or
- either artifact no longer matches the deterministic builder; or
- any of 19 required findings is softened.

The 23 negative self-tests deliberately introduce those failures and require each one to
be rejected.

## 8. Remaining approval and implementation gates

This scenario catalog can support review, but it cannot satisfy the reviews it calls for.
Before any live journey implementation:

1. approve identity, recovery, current-inpatient source, lifecycle, race, and
   representative designs;
2. approve each operation and its authorization/non-disclosure matrix;
3. approve each field's source, release authority, freshness, uncertainty, correction,
   translation, and offline rule;
4. approve patient state/timing vocabulary and all patient-visible copy;
5. approve communication replay, routing, urgency, response, and lifecycle semantics;
6. approve notification provider, payload, consent, permission, revocation, and support
   behavior;
7. approve legal/privacy/clinical sensitive-service and representative policy;
8. complete patient-advisor, accessibility, language/interpreter, and accommodation
   review;
9. implement canonical backend/iOS/Android fixtures in default-off non-production
   integration;
10. run cross-surface transition, outage, security, clinical-hazard, recovery, and rollback
    tests; and
11. obtain a separate pilot go/no-go decision.

## 9. Explicit non-claims

Completion of this candidate means only that the listed synthetic scenarios have a
machine-enforced, internally consistent, default-deny reference outcome. It does not mean:

- Today, My Path, Care Team, education, discharge, communication, or notification exists;
- any clinical content, term, timing statement, source, or patient instruction is correct;
- representative or sensitive-service access is legally or clinically approved;
- production integration, reliability, security, accessibility, or patient comprehension
  has been proven;
- a sample or real patient was created or accessed;
- a distribution artifact, store record, pilot, migration, or deployment is authorized;
  or
- the overall Nightingale product is clinically ratified or complete.
